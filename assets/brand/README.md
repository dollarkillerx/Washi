# Washi brand

## The mark: Fold grid

Four squares make the grid of boxes on a form (マス目). Two of them carry
meaning:

- **Top right** is folded over, so only its lower-left half remains. This is
  the corner of a sheet of paper (和紙).
- **Bottom right** is vermilion. This is the field being filled in, which is
  what Washi does to flat forms.

The look follows design-language principles also used by IBM (Carbon / IBM
Design Language):

- strict grid and right angles
- flat colour, no gradients, shadows or rounded corners
- one accent colour
- IBM Plex type

Washi is not affiliated with IBM and uses none of IBM's marks.

## Construction

The artboard is 512 × 512 on an 8-unit grid. It holds two 168-unit squares
with a 16-unit gutter, inside an 80-unit margin. The drawn part is 352 × 352.
The fold runs from the top-left corner of the top-right square to its
bottom-right corner.

Small icons are not scaled down from the master. Each one is drawn on whole
pixels so its edges stay sharp. All values are pixels:

| size | margin | square | gutter |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | 2 | 5 | 2 |
| 24 | 3 | 8 | 2 |
| 32 | 4 | 11 | 2 |
| 48 | 6 | 17 | 2 |
| 64 | 8 | 22 | 4 |

## Type

- **IBM Plex Sans SemiBold** for "Washi". Regular and Medium for supporting text.
- **IBM Plex Sans JP Regular** for 和紙.

Both fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Every SVG here has
its lettering outlined, so no font is needed to display it. The font files are
not part of this repository.

## Rules

- **Clear space:** at least one square-and-gutter (the width of a square plus
  the gap next to it) on every side of the mark.
- **Minimum size:**
  - the mark: 16 px, using the hinted PNGs
  - the wordmark: 24 px tall
  - the wordmark with 和紙: 32 px tall
- **Do not** rotate the mark, recolour its squares, round its corners, add
  effects, or move the vermilion square.
